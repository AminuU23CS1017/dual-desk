DUAL DESK — WINDOWS MT5 SETUP

Your Deriv website sign-in and your MT5 terminal are separate connections.
Install this on the Windows computer where you run Deriv MT5.
On your phone, open Dual Desk and sign in with the same ChatGPT account.
The phone needs no bridge installation or separate pairing key. Windows and
MT5 must stay running to keep the reports current.

1. Open MT5 and sign in to your Deriv DEMO MT5 account for testing.
2. On Dual Desk, sign in and open Accounts & bots > Windows MT5 setup.
   Download DualDeskBridge.mq5 and click Create pairing key.
   Keep the pairing key private. It connects this terminal to your website account.
3. In MT5: File > Open Data Folder > MQL5 > Experts.
   Copy DualDeskBridge.mq5 into that Experts folder.
4. Press F4 in MT5 to open MetaEditor. Open the copied file, then press F7.
   Compilation must show 0 errors. If there are errors, copy their text for support.
5. Back in MT5: Tools > Options > Expert Advisors.
   Enable WebRequest for the URL below and add it exactly:
   https://dual-desk-trading.ayomideaminu.chatgpt.site
6. In Navigator > Expert Advisors, refresh the list. Drag DualDeskBridge
   onto ONE chart. In Inputs, paste the pairing key into PairingKey.
   Leave AllowDemoAutomation=false for the first connection test.
7. Keep MT5 open. Refresh Dual Desk. Windows MT5 should show Terminal online
   with your account balance and equity within about 15 seconds.

AUTOMATION IS A SEPARATE STEP
After the bridge compiles and the account connection is checked, demo automation
requires all three: MT5 Algo Trading enabled, AllowDemoAutomation=true in the EA,
and Enable MT5 demo bot on the website. The strategy is unvalidated.
Real accounts are view-only; the bridge refuses real-money orders.
Your computer and MT5 must remain running for the bridge to work.
Bridge 1.10 reports today’s trading deals; earlier history is not imported.

BRIDGE 1.10: POSITION AND PROFIT REPORTS
After pairing, open Active trades & P&L on the site. The MT5 section shows
individual positions and today's trading deals in your broker currency.
Reports become stale when Windows/MT5 stops. The day uses your broker's clock.
Up to 50 positions and 500 trading deals per day are reported. An incomplete
report is labelled; no full-day total is claimed. Separate balance adjustments
are excluded. Real accounts are monitoring-only; real orders remain disabled.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- HTTP -1 / MT5 4014: check the WebRequest URL and run this as an Expert Advisor
  on a normal chart, not an indicator or in Strategy Tester.
- HTTP 401: check PairingKey. If you replaced the key, paste the newest key.
- Offline after 45 seconds: check internet, MT5, and Windows sleep settings.
- Compilation errors: save the exact MetaEditor error text for review. This
  source has not been compiled or tested on your Windows terminal yet.
- Leave AllowDemoAutomation=false until reports and symbol details are checked.
- Do not paste your account password, wallet seed phrase or private key here.
